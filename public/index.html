VAMSHAA AGROTECH FOUNDATION is a Section 8 company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, in India, with its registered office at Singrauli, Madhya Pradesh.

Our development approach is based on three principles

  • Environmental sustainability
  • Industrial and economic development
  • Inclusive community participation
About the Foundation

Two sectors, one green economy

Bamboo-based industrial development

Promoting bamboo cultivation, processing, manufacturing, engineering, construction materials, furniture, paper, packaging, textiles, charcoal, biomass, handicrafts and advanced bamboo-based products.

15 bamboo industry areas

Integrated waste management

Promoting scientific waste collection, segregation, material recovery, recycling, composting, biomass utilisation, resource recovery and circular economy solutions.

11 waste management areas
  • Plantation
  • Construction
  • Furniture
  • Handicrafts
  • Segregation
  • Composting

Bamboo and waste, connected in one loop

We aim to connect bamboo industries and waste management through a circular economy model. The model focuses on responsible production, efficient resource utilisation, waste reduction, recycling and value addition.

Our goal is to minimise waste and maximise environmental and economic value.

See the circular model

Our vision

To become a leading contributor to India's sustainable green industrial transformation by developing bamboo-based industries, scientific waste management systems and resource-efficient rural enterprises.

Building a green economy

Through our proposed programmes and partnerships, we aim to contribute towards:

  • Environmental sustainability

    Encouraging responsible resource utilisation and environmental protection.

  • Green industrialisation

    Promoting bamboo manufacturing and resource recovery industries.

  • Employment generation

    Creating opportunities through green enterprises and manufacturing.

  • Rural development

    Strengthening rural economies through local resource-based businesses.

  • Women empowerment

    Encouraging women-led enterprises and skill development.

  • Circular economy

    Promoting recycling, reuse and material recovery.

  • Technology development

    Encouraging research, innovation and modern manufacturing.

  • Community participation

    Building awareness and collective responsibility for environmental protection.
