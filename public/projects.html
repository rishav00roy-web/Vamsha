Our impact & development objectives

Through our proposed programmes and partnerships, we aim to contribute towards:

  • Environmental sustainability

    Encouraging responsible resource utilisation and environmental protection.

  • Green industrialisation

    Promoting bamboo manufacturing and resource recovery industries.

  • Employment generation

    Creating opportunities through green enterprises and manufacturing.

  • Rural development

    Strengthening rural economies through local resource-based businesses.

  • Women empowerment

    Encouraging women-led enterprises and skill development.

  • Circular economy

    Promoting recycling, reuse and material recovery.

  • Technology development

    Encouraging research, innovation and modern manufacturing.

  • Community participation

    Building awareness and collective responsibility for environmental protection.
